Lexicon

The lexicon holds 528 words: 486 ordinary words and 42 holy words.

The classes are n noun, pron pronoun, v verb, adj adjective, num numeral, adv adverb, and part particle. Holy words show in the accent color.

A script chose most forms with a fixed random process. The forms follow the sound rules, but they do not come from older words. The ring column shows the same spelling in the ring script. Nouns, pronouns, verbs, and adjectives take affixes: read the list of affixes.
