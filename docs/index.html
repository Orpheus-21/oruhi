A constructed language

A language for a people who worship zero.

Oruhi has 528 words, 36 syllables, a script made of rings, and a number system in base 8. This site holds the full technical description.

The people of Oruhi live in a river valley. A great desert lies at one end of the valley. They believe that all things come from empty space and return to it. They call this space Oru, which means zero.

Oruo

Oru-o    zero-HON

The word for zero with the ending of address: "O Zero." The empty ring at the start and at the end is the vowel o. Only holy words contain this vowel.

The Prayer of the Empty

The prayer starts at the center and turns clockwise. The Keepers of the Empty write holy texts in this way. Each ring is one syllable.

Eight facts about Oruhi

The number of facts is 10 in base 8. The people write it with two rings: 10.

  1. 1
    Eight consonants and four vowels. The consonants are p t k m n s h r. The vowels are a i u o. Every syllable is a vowel with an optional consonant before it.
  2. 2
    The vowel o is holy. A word contains o only if the word is holy. Ordinary words use a, i, and u. Holy words start with a capital letter.
  3. 3
    The script is a ring. The ring is zero. A tick outside the ring names the consonant. A mark inside the ring names the vowel. A ring with no mark is the vowel o.
  4. 4
    Numbers use base 8. The people count the gaps between their fingers, not the fingers. Four gaps on each hand give eight.
  5. 5
    Every zero is spoken. A number is read digit by digit. The number 8 is written 10 in base 8, and the people say kisa Oru, "one zero".
  6. 6
    The copula is zero. The language has no verb for "to be". The sentence sun bright means "The sun is bright."
  7. 7
    The empty ending is the holiest form. The basic form of a word has no ending. A prayer keeps the empty ending wherever the grammar allows it.
  8. 10
    Latin spelling is the ring script. This site holds only Latin letters. A font draws the letters as rings. Select any ring text, and you copy the Latin spelling.

Read one sentence

Each sentence has five lines. The first line is the ring script. The next three lines give the spelling, the parts, and the gloss of each word. The last line is the English translation.

The verb punupi has no ending, so the sentence is timeless. The ending -ni marks the thing that the verb acts on.
