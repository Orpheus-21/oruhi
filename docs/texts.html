Texts

Three texts show the language in use: a myth, a prayer, and a dialogue.

Each sentence has four layers. First comes the ring line. Under it, each word shows its spelling, its parts, and its gloss. The English translation comes last. Small capitals are affix labels: the grammar page lists them all.

The check script composed each sentence from its gloss and parsed it again. Every word has one reading.
