@ Grammar examples
% One short sentence for each rule of the grammar.

# word order and the zero copula
> sun bright
= The sun is bright.
> woman water-ACC drink
= The woman drinks water.
> child bread-ACC eat-PAST
= The child ate the bread.
> I river-ACC cross-FUT
= I will cross the river.

# plural
> dog-PL run
= The dogs run.
> three dog run
= Three dogs run. The noun stays singular after a number.
> I-PL walk-FUT
= We will walk.

# cases
> man child-DAT bread-ACC give-PAST
= The man gave bread to the child.
> I knife-INS bread-ACC cut-PAST
= I cut the bread with a knife.
> woman house-LOC sleep
= The woman sleeps in the house.
> bird tree-ABL fall-PAST
= The bird fell from the tree.
> I friend-COM walk-PAST
= I walked with a friend.
> mother-GEN house big
= The house of the mother is big.

# adjectives and adverbs
> woman big house-ACC build-PAST
= The woman built a big house.
> river deep-PAST
= The river was deep.
> I always here sleep
= I always sleep here.

# joining clauses
> I bread-ACC eat and water-ACC drink
= I eat bread and drink water.
> you fish-ACC or meat-ACC want q
= Do you want fish or meat?
> rain-FUT if I house-LOC sit-FUT
= If it rains, I will sit in the house.
> hunger because I bread-ACC eat-PAST
= I ate bread because I was hungry.

# negation, command, question
> I fish-ACC eat-NEG
= I do not eat fish.
> river flow-PAST-NEG
= The river did not flow.
> water-ACC drink-IMP
= Drink the water!
> you fish-ACC want q
= Do you want fish?
> who river-ACC cross-PAST
= Who crossed the river?
> this what
= What is this?

# words made from verbs
> hunt-AGENT wolf-ACC kill-PAST
= The hunter killed the wolf.
> sing-NMLZ good
= The singing is good.
> teach-AGENT-GEN house far
= The house of the teacher is far.

# the holy register
> zero empty
= Zero is empty.
> zero-GEN guard-AGENT pilgrim-ACC welcome-PAST
= The Keeper of the Empty welcomed the pilgrim.
> zero-HON
= O Zero.
> zero you-ACC protect
= Zero protects you. The verb has no ending, so the sentence is timeless and holy.

# numbers are read digit by digit, and every zero is spoken
> one zero person walk
= Eight people walk. The digits one and zero make 10 in base 8, which is eight.
