@ The Prayer of the Empty
% The Keepers say this prayer at dawn. Holy words keep the empty ending where the grammar allows it.

> zero-HON
= O Zero.
> zero-HON you empty
= O Zero, you are empty.
> zero-HON you endless
= O Zero, you are endless.
> zero-HON I-ACC protect-IMP
= O Zero, protect me.
> zero-HON I-GEN sin-PL-ACC forgive-IMP
= O Zero, forgive my sins.
> I-GEN heart-ACC empty_out-IMP
= Empty out my heart.
> silent-NMLZ-INS I-ACC fill-IMP
= Fill me with silence.
> I-GEN breath void-DAT return
= My breath returns to the Void.
> zero-HON
= O Zero.
